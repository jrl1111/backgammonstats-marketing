Analytics built for backgammon

From performance rating trends to head-to-head matchup prep, every feature is designed around how serious players actually study the game.

📈

Performance Tracking

Watch your PR improve over time with rolling averages, skill tier progression, and trend analysis across 10, 25, 50, 100+ match windows.

🎯

Opponent Prep

Auto-generated Game Plan briefings for every rival. Review head-to-head records, cube tendencies, and personal notes before your next match.

🎲

Luck vs Skill Analysis

Separate your pure skill wins from lucky ones. Track luck differentials, detect variance patterns, and see your true strength.

🎥

Video + Notes

Link YouTube videos to matches. Add timestamped notes as you review. Build a personal scouting report for every opponent.

🗓️

Playing Patterns

Discover when you play best. Day-of-week performance, monthly trends, streaks, and activity heat maps reveal your rhythm.

🏆

Skill Tier Progression

Benchmark against industry-standard PR tiers: World Class, Grandmaster, Expert, Advanced, Intermediate, Beginner. Know exactly where you stand.

Three steps

Online or over-the-board — every match counts

Whether you're grinding online or rolling dice across a real board, BackgammonStats captures every match and turns it into insight.

1

Upload all your match files

Drag and drop every match file you've got — Backgammon Galaxy, Heroes, XG, gnubg, or live transcriptions — and store them in one central place. No more scattered folders, lost files, or juggling formats. Your entire match history, online and over-the-board, lives together in a single searchable library.

2

Explore your data

Every match — online or live — flows into one unified dashboard. Filter by site, opponent, match length, or venue, and compare how your game holds up across formats.

3

Level up your game

Track PR trends, prep for rivals you've faced across a table or a screen, and turn every match — wherever it's played — into your next edge.
